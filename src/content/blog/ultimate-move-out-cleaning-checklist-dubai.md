# The Ultimate Move-Out Cleaning Checklist for Dubai Tenants

Moving to a new home is exciting, but it also comes with a long list of responsibilities. One of the most important tasks is preparing your current property before handing it back to the landlord or property manager.

A detailed move-out cleaning helps present the property in good condition and can make the handover process smoother.

This checklist covers the main areas that many tenants review before leaving an apartment or villa in Dubai.

---

# Why Move-Out Cleaning Matters

A property that has been cleaned thoroughly is generally easier for landlords or property managers to inspect.

Move-out cleaning is commonly arranged before:

- Lease completion
- Selling a property
- Property handover
- New tenant occupancy
- Property management inspections

Always review your tenancy agreement to understand any cleaning obligations that may apply.

---

# Whole Property Checklist

Walk through every room and check:

- Dust on surfaces
- Floors
- Walls (where appropriate)
- Doors
- Door frames
- Light switches
- Skirting boards
- Internal windows
- Mirrors
- Waste bins
- Accessible cupboards

---

# Kitchen Checklist

Kitchen areas often require the most attention.

Review:

- Countertops
- Sink
- Splashback
- Cabinet exteriors
- Appliance exteriors
- Floor
- Walls near cooking areas
- Windows
- Doors
- Handles

If appliance cleaning is included in your service, confirm this before booking.

---

# Bathroom Checklist

Bathrooms should be checked carefully.

Review:

- Toilet
- Washbasin
- Shower
- Bathtub
- Mirrors
- Countertops
- Floor
- Walls
- Tiles
- Accessible shelving

---

# Bedrooms

Check:

- Floors
- Wardrobes
- Doors
- Mirrors
- Internal windows
- Skirting boards
- Light switches

---

# Living Room

Review:

- Furniture (if remaining)
- Floors
- Internal windows
- Doors
- TV unit
- Shelves
- Dust accumulation
- Ceiling corners

---

# Balcony

Where applicable:

- Sweep floors
- Remove dust
- Clean railings
- Wipe accessible glass
- Remove rubbish

---

# Furniture

If furniture remains in the property, consider:

- Sofa cleaning
- Mattress cleaning
- Curtain cleaning
- Carpet cleaning
- Upholstery cleaning

Professional furniture cleaning may be booked separately depending on your requirements.

---

# Before the Final Inspection

Take a final walkthrough.

Check:

- Lights switched off
- Windows closed
- Waste removed
- Personal belongings removed
- Keys ready
- Cleaning completed

Photographs can also help document the property's condition before handover.

---

# Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many tenants forget to clean:

- Behind accessible furniture
- Door frames
- Skirting boards
- Light switches
- Balcony areas
- Kitchen cabinet exteriors
- Internal glass
- Storage areas

Using a checklist helps reduce the chance of missing important areas.
