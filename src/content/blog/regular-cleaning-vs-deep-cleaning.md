Many Dubai homeowners wonder whether they need regular cleaning or a more detailed deep-cleaning appointment.

Although both services improve the condition of a property, they have different purposes.

## What Is Regular Cleaning?

Regular cleaning focuses on routine household maintenance.

It may include:

- Dusting accessible surfaces
- Vacuuming carpets
- Sweeping and mopping floors
- Cleaning kitchen countertops
- Cleaning bathrooms
- Emptying bins
- General tidying

## What Is Deep Cleaning?

Deep cleaning provides more detailed attention to areas that may not be addressed during routine housekeeping.

It may include:

- Skirting boards
- Doors and frames
- Detailed kitchen cleaning
- Bathroom deep cleaning
- Accessible areas behind furniture
- Internal glass
- Detailed dust removal

## Which Service Should You Choose?

Choose regular cleaning when your property is already maintained and needs ongoing support.

Choose deep cleaning when the property requires more time, attention, and detailed work.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does deep cleaning replace regular cleaning?

No. Deep cleaning provides detailed attention, while regular cleaning helps maintain the results.

### How often should I arrange deep cleaning?

That depends on the property's occupancy, condition, size, lifestyle, and cleaning routine.
