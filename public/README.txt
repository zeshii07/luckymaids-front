Place these generated files in your Vite project's public/ folder:
- site.webmanifest
- robots.txt
- browserconfig.xml

Also place your favicon files in public/:
- favicon.ico
- favicon-16x16.png
- favicon-32x32.png
- apple-touch-icon.png
- android-chrome-192x192.png
- android-chrome-512x512.png

Optional:
- mstile-150x150.png (only needed if you keep browserconfig.xml)

Paste index-head-snippet.html inside the <head> of the root index.html.

Replace https://www.luckycrystalmaids.com if your final domain is different.
